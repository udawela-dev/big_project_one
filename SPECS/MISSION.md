# FreshTrack — Mission

## Why do we exist?

People forget what food they have at home and when it expires, so usable food
gets thrown away. FreshTrack fixes that with a simple list of the food you own
and its expiry dates, so you always know what to eat before it goes bad.

## Who is it for?

Students, families, and households who want to stop wasting food.

## What does success look like?

The app is used every week.

## Non-negotiables

- Keep the data simple — each food has just a name and an expiry date.
- The app must be easy for beginners to read and change.
- No accounts, no linking to shops, no extra features that distract from the
  core list.

## Out of scope

- Logins or user accounts (no accounts).
- Connecting to shops or online stores (no linking to shops).
